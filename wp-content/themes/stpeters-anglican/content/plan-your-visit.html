What to expect

Services last about an hour. We worship with two prayer books — the Book of Common Prayer and the Book of Alternative Services — both handed to you at the door, so there's nothing to prepare and no need to know your way around.

Sit anywhere you like and follow along as much or as little as you're comfortable with. After the service someone will say hello — and you're welcome to chat, or to slip away just as quietly.

Good to know

Accessibility

There's level access and a wheelchair entrance, accessible washrooms, and parking close to the door. We're glad to help however we can — just ask.

Families and children

Children are very welcome at worship. There's space to move around, and families come with kids of all ages — come exactly as you are.

Come as you are

There's no dress code and no church-going test. Whether you've been coming for years or this is your first time, there's a seat for you.

After the service

There's coffee and conversation after worship — a slow, easy way to meet people and ask questions.

Any questions?

We'd love to help you feel ready before you arrive. Call the office or send a note, and we'll answer whatever's on your mind.
